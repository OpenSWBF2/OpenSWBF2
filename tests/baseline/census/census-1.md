## Lower bound

This census is a lower bound. On x64, `operator new(unsigned int)` is not a valid allocation function, so most game TUs fail with C2821 and the errors that cascade from it. cl stops early in those TUs, before it reaches most front-end warnings, so their C4311/C4312/C4302/C4267/C4244 sites are undercounted. The counts will rise once Phase 22 (PTR-01) lets those TUs compile. Census #2 must compare like-for-like TUs (use the TU list in `census-1-tu.csv`). No numeric target is set.
